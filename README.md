# NEON-EMPIRE
AI-assisted original open-world game concept and cinematic promotional trailer.
NEON EMPIRE

Build Your Empire. Own the Streets. Rule Your Destiny.

🌆 About the Game

NEON EMPIRE is set in Vicehaven, a fictional coastal metropolis where luxury, ambition, underground crime networks and rival gangs collide.

The concept follows Adrian Cross, a 29-year-old mechanic who returns to his hometown and becomes involved in a dangerous world of power, betrayal and survival.

🎮 Key Features

- Open-world city exploration
- Luxury vehicles and street racing
- Nightlife and casino environments
- Rival gangs and action sequences
- Character-driven storytelling
- Cinematic urban atmosphere
